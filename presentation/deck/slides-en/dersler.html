What we learned

1

The model is not deterministic; the checks around it must be.

2

Give concrete errors: file, line, the server's answer.

3

Keep the good state: roll back a fix that breaks things.

4

Every error becomes a test: no error comes twice.

5

Ask a human when unsure: instead of guessing.
